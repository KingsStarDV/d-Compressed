# d-Compressed 

A compressed Air based projectile shooter, capable to be configured as rifle or marksman. Required few special parts and rest is everyday part. 

### Why I Made This? 

I always wanted to make a compressed air based shooter. I tried to start many time but always was blocked with a thought in mind that its too !COMPLEX! 

So, i tried and made a simpler one.

## Assembly Instructions 

1. First steps are easy and self explainatory from the cad image-
 
   <img width="371" height="329" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-02 220739" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8233fa90-c8e4-445e-8667-fcc653261075" />
   
   [leave the part after the elbow joint for now]
   Assemble all those part together with ptfe tape to secure joints.

2. Now for the part after the elbow joint-
   Take piece of hollow rod about 5-6cm long and fix it in the end of the elbow joint with help of epoxy putty.
   then take a piece which fits tightly inside the hollow rod but still slides in it and put it inside of the rod.

3. Then attach a servo motor to move the sliding part.
